Privacy Policy

¡Vamos! Spanish for Kids · Last updated 23 October 2026

The short version: ¡Vamos! collects nothing. No personal information, no usage data, no device identifiers. Nothing leaves your child's device.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no third-party code (no SDKs for tracking, advertising or crash reporting).

What stays on the device

Your child's progress (stamps, words learned, settings, and the first name they type in, if any) is saved only on the device, inside the app. We can't see it. Deleting the app deletes it, and the grown-ups area has a "Start over" button that erases it.

Network use

The app works fully offline and makes no network requests. The guide voices are recorded and built into the app; no speech is sent anywhere. If you choose the device's own voice in the grown-ups area, speech is produced by your device's built-in system voices.

Purchases

The optional Passport purchase is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. The purchase sits behind a parental gate.

Children's privacy

¡Vamos! is made for children and is designed to comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), GDPR-K and Apple's Kids Category rules. Because we collect no data, there is nothing to review, share or delete on our side.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, we will update this page and the date above before the change takes effect in the app.
